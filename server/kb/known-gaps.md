## Things the knowledge base does not cover
keywords: gap, unknown, not covered

The assistant does not have the following information and must not guess it. It should say it does not know and offer the human handoff:
- The full ingredient list (INCI) beyond 2% Kojic Acid and Vitamin E, and allergen information.
- Shelf life, expiry, storage instructions and batch details.
- Patch-test instructions, use during pregnancy or breastfeeding, use with other actives or medications, and use by children.
- Clinical study results or percentages of users who saw results.
- The exact free-shipping threshold, discount codes, offers and loyalty programmes.
- Order tracking, order status, payment problems and account issues for a specific order.
- International shipping.
- Other products. The site's shop page lists only Eyecojic Under Eye Gel as confirmed; other listings are placeholders and must not be described.
- Comparisons with other brands or products.
