## About Eyecojic and RLS
keywords: brand, company, about, who makes, rls, history, 1973, ophthalmic, eye care, prayagraj, noida, founded, trusted

Eyecojic comes from a legacy of eye care that dates back to 1973. The site describes RLS as a trusted name in ophthalmic care in India, started in 1973, with a pan-India presence, more than 250 employees, a head office in Prayagraj and a corporate office in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The Eyecojic formula is described as developed through research with specialists and scientists who work on eye issues.
