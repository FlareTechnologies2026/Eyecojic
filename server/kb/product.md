## What Eyecojic Under Eye Gel is
keywords: product, gel, under eye, dark circles, what does it do, benefits, brightening, cooling, texture

Eyecojic Under Eye Gel is a light, cooling gel formulated with 2% Kojic Acid and Vitamin E. It is made to reduce the appearance of dark circles, brighten the under-eye area and nourish the delicate skin around the eyes. The texture is lightweight and absorbs quickly without a sticky residue.

## Key ingredients
keywords: ingredients, kojic acid, vitamin e, formula, paraben, vegan, cruelty free, natural, tyrosinase, melanin, antioxidant

The gel contains 2% Kojic Acid and Vitamin E. Kojic Acid is described on the site as a targeted ingredient that helps fade the look of dark under-eye circles. Vitamin E is an antioxidant that helps protect the delicate skin around the eyes. The formula is paraben-free, vegan and cruelty-free.

## How to use
keywords: how to use, apply, application, routine, morning, evening, night, pea sized, ring finger, tap, directions

Dot a pea-sized amount onto your ring finger. Gently tap it around the under-eye area from the inner corner to the outer corner, then let it absorb completely. Use it in the morning and evening on clean skin.

Most customers notice a visible difference within 2 to 4 weeks of consistent daily use, using it morning and night as part of a regular routine. Individual results vary.
