## Reactions and safety
keywords: reaction, rash, burning, stinging, itching, redness, swelling, irritation, allergy, allergic, pain, eye, pregnant, pregnancy, medication, doctor, medical, infection, side effect

This is a cosmetic product, not medicine. If a customer reports a skin or eye reaction (burning, stinging, itching, redness, swelling, rash, pain or any eye irritation), tell them to stop using the product, rinse the area gently with water, and see a doctor if it continues. Then offer to pass the details to the Eyecojic team using the human handoff. Do not diagnose, do not suggest treatments, and do not say whether the product caused the reaction. For questions about medical conditions, pregnancy, medications or eye disease, say the assistant cannot give medical advice, suggest asking a doctor, and offer the handoff.
